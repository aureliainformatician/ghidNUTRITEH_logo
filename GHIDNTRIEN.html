Macronutrients, Caloric Energy, Vitamins, and Minerals (Ages 10-18)

Proteins (P)
1 gram = 4 kcal

Building blocks of muscles and tissues. Rebuild body cells.

Carbohydrates (C)
1 gram = 4 kcal

Primary fast fuel for the brain, learning, and physical effort.

Fats / Lipids (F)
1 gram = 9 kcal

Concentrated energy reserve and thermal insulation for organs.

General Calorie Calculation Formula:

Total Calories (kcal) = (Proteins g × 4) + (Carbs g × 4) + (Fats g × 9)
